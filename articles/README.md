# Wire-bond defects, from first principles to AI

An open, beginner-friendly series on **wire bonding** in semiconductor packaging: how it works, how
it fails, how defects are inspected, and how AI can help detect them. It is the background to
[BondSight](../README.md), the open-source AOI for wire bonds.

Written by **Dr. Avishek Mondal**, materials scientist with hands-on experience in semiconductor
packaging (die attach, wire bonding, encapsulation, yield and defect analysis). Every claim has a
source; short versions of each part are in [`linkedin/`](../linkedin/).
